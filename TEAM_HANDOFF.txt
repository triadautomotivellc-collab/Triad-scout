WHAT TRIAD SCOUT IS BECOMING
=================================================================

TRIAD Scout should become one automotive sourcing intelligence layer.

The user searches ONCE.

TRIAD Scout searches every appropriate enabled source.

It then:
- normalizes listings
- removes duplicates
- estimates real acquisition cost
- ranks opportunities

- links back to the original listing for verification

3. CRAIGSLIST
-------------

Craigslist must be supported for BOTH vehicles and parts where public access allows.

Important categories include:
- cars & trucks
- auto parts
- wheels / tires

Craigslist is MARKET / LOCATION oriented.

TRIAD Scout should search relevant surrounding markets based on the user's origin and radius instead of treating Craigslist as one national database.

Do not build an anti-bot bypass system.
Use reasonable, permitted access methods and gracefully report when a market/source cannot be searched.

=================================================================
TOTAL ACQUISITION COST
=================================================================

Do NOT rank inventory by sticker price alone.

Example:

PART PRICE          $700
SHIPPING            $250
CORE                $150
-------------------------
EST. LANDED COST  $1,100

A $700 transmission hundreds of miles away may be worse than an $850 unit nearby.

For complete vehicles consider:
- purchase/bid price
- auction fees
- buyer fees
- transport
- title fees
- estimated repair
- likely retail
- projected gross margin
